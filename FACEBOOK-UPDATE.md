UPDATE - THERE IS NOW A SECOND SCAM GOING AROUND

A colleague forwarded me this one during the week. It pretends to be Services Australia and says your PRODA account needs to be "re-linked" to keep using HPOS, with a Reconnect PRODA button.

It is a different attack to the file-share one. It does not install anything on your computer - it just steals your PRODA login, which reaches Medicare billing, DVA, the immunisation register and patient records.

The tell is simple: the button does not go to a .gov.au address. In this one it goes to a Spanish website. Even the text in the email that reads "servicesaustralia.gov.au" is a link to that same fake site, so reading the words and trusting them is the trap.

The rule: never reach PRODA through a link in an email. Type the address yourself or use your bookmark. And do not ring the phone number in the email either - the scam prints its own.

I have updated the site so the two scams are now side by side with what to do for each, because the responses are different. Wiping a computer does nothing about a stolen PRODA login, and changing a password does nothing about an infected machine.
